📡 Webhook Verification & Configuration

Meta sends real-time message delivery receipts (Delivered, Read, Failed) and incoming chats to this endpoint.

For localhost testing, use your public tunnel URL (e.g. ngrok or Cloudflare tunnel).

Secure random token (rendered as ********) checked by GET /api/whatsapp/webhook

Step-by-Step Meta Webhook Instructions:
  1. Log in to Meta for Developers and select your App.
  2. In the left sidebar, navigate to WhatsApp > Configuration.
  3. Locate the Callback URL section and click Edit.
  4. Paste the Callback URL and Verify Token shown above.
  5. Click Verify and Save. Meta will send a GET handshake request to your endpoint and verify it instantly!
  6. Under Webhook fields, click Manage, find messages, and click Subscribe.
